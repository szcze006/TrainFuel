## Sprint 2 Conclusion

Sprint 2 successfully delivered the core functionality and primary user interfaces for TrainFUEL. The team completed Firebase Authentication, Firebase nutrition and workout tracking, profile tracking, and the primary Fitness, Login, Profile, Nutrition, and Home interfaces.

The sprint also delivered application navigation. During development, coding errors and Firebase integration difficulties created blockers that required additional debugging and testing.

The original sprint commitment consisted of 8 issues totaling 29 story points. Two additional issues totaling 10 story points were added during the sprint, resulting in a final scope of 10 issues and 39 story points.

All 10 issues and all 39 story points were completed by the end of Sprint 2.
